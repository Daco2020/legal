Privacy policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Weuse ("we") respects the privacy of people who use Hadaho ("the app"), a skin retouching app. We publish this policy under Article 30 of Korea's Personal Information Protection Act.

Hadaho retouches photos and videos only on your phone. The photos and videos you pick, and any face information, are never sent to us. There is no sign-up, no advertising, and no tracking. The app sends us only daily usage counts, such as how many photos were saved, and they carry no ID that identifies you or your device. Apart from those counts, we receive only the emails you choose to send us.

1. What we collect and how

CategoryItemsHow it's collected
Usage statisticsHow many times a day each app feature was used: photos and videos saved (counted by retouch level), items left as originals because no face was found, failed retouches or saves, retouches you stopped, comparisons with the original, app opens, times the payment screen opened (after the free retouches ran out, or from the menu), and purchases. The app also counts the first time you open it each day, once per device, along with how long ago the app was first downloaded, as one of these ranges: same day, 1 day, 2 to 6 days, 7 to 13 days, 14 to 29 days, 30 days or more, or unknown. Usage statistics carry no ID that identifies you or your device. They never include photos, videos, face information, names, contact details, age, or gender. To keep a batch from being counted twice, the app attaches a random number to it. That number is new every time the app sends, has nothing to do with you or your device, and is deleted from our server after 7 days. Apart from that number, the server keeps only totals by date, app version, country, device model, and feature (with the download range or save milestone where one applies), with no record of any one person. Cloudflare's network receives your IP address while it passes the request along, but we don't store or view it (request logging is turned off).

Emailing us is optional. You can use every part of the app without it. Please leave out details we don't need to answer you, such as your phone number.

What we don't collect: photos, videos, face information, names, phone numbers, age, gender, account information, location, contacts, advertising identifiers, any ID that identifies you or your device, or payment information. We don't use advertising tools or third-party analytics.

What stays on your phone

  • Photos and videos: The app reads only the items you pick in the photo picker. It never asks for access to your whole photo library.

While retouching, it keeps a copy of each video in the app's temporary folder and deletes it when you clear the list.

  • Face detection: Used only to find areas of skin. It doesn't identify anyone, and face data isn't stored.
  • Saving: Retouched results are added to Photos as new copies. For this, the app asks for permission to add photos. Your originals are never changed.
  • App settings: The retouch level you chose, and whether the app has shown you the guide (How it works), are stored only on your phone.
  • Usage counts: Each day's counts are kept on your phone until the app sends them.
  • Last open and save total: The date you last opened the app, and how many photos and videos you've saved on this device in total, are stored only on your phone. The app uses them only to count the first open of each day and each save milestone once. They're never sent to us.
  • Lifetime pass: Apple handles payment through the App Store. We never receive your card number, other payment details, or Apple Account information. To confirm your purchase, the app checks Apple's signed purchase record on your phone.
  • Free retouch count: The number of photos and videos you've retouched and saved for free, and whether you can use the app free for life, are stored only in your phone's keychain. They're never sent to us.

2. Why we use it

  • Usage statistics: To learn which features people use most and where things fail, so we can fix and improve the app. They also show how many devices use the app each day and whether people come back to it.
  • Email inquiries: To answer your questions, and to fix problems and improve the app based on what you tell us.

We use your email address only to reply to you.

We don't use this information for advertising, for profiling, or to track you across other companies' apps and websites. We never sell it under any circumstances.

3. How long we keep it

Usage statistics are totals that can't identify anyone, so we keep them for as long as we run the service. The random number attached to each batch is deleted after 7 days.

We keep email inquiries for 2 years from the day we receive them, then delete them. If you ask us to delete them sooner, we delete them right away.

4. Sharing with third parties

We don't share your information with third parties.

6. Your rights and how to exercise them

You can ask us at any time to access, correct, delete, or stop processing your personal information. Email weuse.support@gmail.com, and we'll handle your request and tell you the result within 10 days of receiving it.

Because usage statistics can't identify anyone, we can't find or delete the records of any particular person.

App settings, temporary files, and the last-open date and save total on your phone are deleted when you delete the app. The free retouch count and the free-for-life record are an exception. They're kept in the keychain, so they stay on your phone even after you delete the app. That way, reinstalling the app doesn't start the count over. Retouched copies saved to Photos are your own photos, so they stay even if you delete the app. You can delete them yourself in Photos.

7. How we destroy data

When the retention period ends or the purpose has been met, we destroy the personal information without delay. Electronic files are permanently deleted so they can't be recovered, and we don't keep printed copies.

8. Security measures

  • On-device processing: All photo and video analysis and retouching happen on your phone. Photos and videos are never sent to us
  • Minimal collection: The app sends us only daily usage counts, with no ID that identifies you or your device, and the server keeps only totals. Beyond that, we receive only the emails you send
  • There are no sign-ups or accounts, so there is no account information to leak

9. Children under 14

We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 14, and we don't offer sign-up. If a child has emailed us, a parent or guardian can let us know at weuse.support@gmail.com and we'll delete it right away.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular where you live or work or where you think the violation happened.
  • Providing information: No law or contract requires you to provide any information. Emailing us is optional. The app sends usage statistics on its own, and they can't identify you.
