Privacy policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Weuse ("we") respects the privacy of people who use Hadaho ("the app"), a skin retouching app. We publish this policy under Article 30 of Korea's Personal Information Protection Act.

Hadaho retouches photos and videos only on your phone. The photos and videos you pick, and any face information, are never sent to us. There is no sign-up, no advertising, and no tracking. The app itself sends us nothing. We receive only the emails you choose to send us.

1. What we collect and how

CategoryItemsHow it's collected
Email inquiriesThe address you send from, what you write, and the app version, iOS version, and device model already filled in the emailWhen you email us from your mail app, for example through Contact us in the menu

Emailing us is optional. You can use every part of the app without it. Please leave out details we don't need to answer you, such as your phone number.

What we don't collect: photos, videos, face information, names, phone numbers, account information, location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or payment information. We don't use advertising tools or third-party analytics.

What stays on your phone

  • Photos and videos: The app reads only the items you pick in the photo picker. It never asks for access to your whole photo library.

While retouching, it keeps a copy of each video in the app's temporary folder and deletes it when you clear the list.

  • Face detection: Used only to find areas of skin. It doesn't identify anyone, and face data isn't stored.
  • Saving: Retouched results are added to Photos as new copies. For this, the app asks for permission to add photos. Your originals are never changed.
  • App settings: The retouch level you chose, and whether the app has shown you the guide (How it works), are stored only on your phone.
  • Lifetime pass: Apple handles payment through the App Store. We never receive your card number, other payment details, or Apple Account information. To confirm your purchase, the app checks Apple's signed purchase record on your phone.
  • Free retouch count: The number of photos and videos you've retouched and saved for free, and whether you can use the app free for life, are stored only in your phone's keychain. They're never sent to us.

2. Why we use it

  • Email inquiries: To answer your questions, and to fix problems and improve the app based on what you tell us.

We use your email address only to reply to you.

We don't use this information for advertising, for profiling, or to track you across other companies' apps and websites. We never sell it under any circumstances.

3. How long we keep it

We keep email inquiries for 2 years from the day we receive them, then delete them. If you ask us to delete them sooner, we delete them right away.

4. Sharing with third parties

We don't share your information with third parties.

6. Your rights and how to exercise them

You can ask us at any time to access, correct, delete, or stop processing your personal information. Email weuse.support@gmail.com, and we'll handle your request and tell you the result within 10 days of receiving it.

App settings and temporary files on your phone are deleted when you delete the app. The free retouch count and the free-for-life record are an exception. They're kept in the keychain, so they stay on your phone even after you delete the app. That way, reinstalling the app doesn't start the count over. Retouched copies saved to Photos are your own photos, so they stay even if you delete the app. You can delete them yourself in Photos.

7. How we destroy data

When the retention period ends or the purpose has been met, we destroy the personal information without delay. Electronic files are permanently deleted so they can't be recovered, and we don't keep printed copies.

8. Security measures

  • On-device processing: All photo and video analysis and retouching happen on your phone, and nothing is sent to us
  • Minimal collection: The app sends us nothing. We receive only the emails you send
  • There are no sign-ups or accounts, so there is no account information to leak

9. Children under 14

We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 14, and we don't offer sign-up. If a child has emailed us, a parent or guardian can let us know at weuse.support@gmail.com and we'll delete it right away.

Make requests the same way as in section 6. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular where you live or work or where you think the violation happened.

  • Automated decisions: We don't use automated decision-making or profiling.
