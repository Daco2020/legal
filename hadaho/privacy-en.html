Privacy policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Weuse ("we") respects the privacy of people who use Hadaho ("the app"), a skin retouching app. We publish this policy under Article 30 of Korea's Personal Information Protection Act.

Hadaho retouches photos and videos only on your phone. The photos and videos you pick, and any face information, are never sent to us. There is no sign-up, no advertising, and no tracking. The only information we receive is the survey answers and emails you choose to send.

1. What we collect and how

CategoryItemsHow it's collected
Survey answersYour rating (Good, Just okay, or Not great), any comment you write, an email address if you choose to add one, app name and version, device model (e.g. iPhone14,5), iOS version, language and region settingsWhen you send the survey, which appears once after you finish retouching for the second time

The survey and email are both optional. You can use every part of the app without sending either. Please don't include personal details such as your name or phone number in your comments.

What we don't collect: photos, videos, face information, names, phone numbers, account information, location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or payment information. We don't use advertising tools or third-party analytics.

What stays on your phone

  • Photos and videos: The app reads only the items you pick in the photo picker. It never asks for access to your whole photo library.

While retouching, it keeps a copy of each video in the app's temporary folder and deletes it when you clear the list.

  • Face detection: Used only to find areas of skin. It doesn't identify anyone, and face data isn't stored.
  • Saving: Retouched results are added to Photos as new copies. For this, the app asks for permission to add photos. Your originals are never changed.
  • App settings: The retouch level you chose, and whether the app has shown you the guide (How it works) and the survey, are stored only on your phone.

2. Why we use it

  • Survey answers and email inquiries: To fix problems and improve the app based on what you tell us, and to answer your questions.

If you leave an email address, we contact you only for the interview or reply you asked for.

  • Connection data: To stop the survey from being flooded with submissions.

We don't use this information for advertising, for profiling, or to track you across other companies' apps and websites. We never sell it under any circumstances.

3. How long we keep it

We keep survey answers and email inquiries for 2 years from the day we receive them, then delete them. If you ask us to delete them sooner, we delete them right away.

We don't store connection data (IP addresses). Cloudflare runs the relay server. Any records it processes on its own for security and service operations are covered by the Cloudflare privacy policy.

4. Sharing with third parties

We don't share your information with third parties.

6. Your rights and how to exercise them

You can ask us at any time to access, correct, delete, or stop processing your personal information. Email weuse.support@gmail.com, and we'll handle your request and tell you the result within 10 days of receiving it.

Survey answers carry no number that identifies your device, so we can't tell who sent them. To have your answers deleted, tell us roughly when you sent them and what they said, and we'll find and delete them.

App settings and temporary files on your phone are deleted when you delete the app. Retouched copies saved to Photos are your own photos, so they stay even if you delete the app. You can delete them yourself in Photos.

7. How we destroy data

When the retention period ends or the purpose has been met, we destroy the personal information without delay. Electronic files are permanently deleted so they can't be recovered, and we don't keep printed copies.

8. Security measures

  • On-device processing: All photo and video analysis and retouching happen on your phone, and nothing is sent to us
  • Minimal collection: Surveys carry no number that identifies your device, and we get an email address only if you type one in
  • Encryption in transit: Surveys are encrypted with HTTPS at every step of transmission
  • Restricted access: The address where surveys are stored is kept as a secret on the relay server only, so it can't be pulled out of the app. Only we can see survey answers
  • There are no sign-ups or accounts, so there is no account information to leak

9. Children under 14

We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 14, and we don't offer sign-up. If a child has sent a survey or email, a parent or guardian can let us know at weuse.support@gmail.com and we'll delete it right away.
