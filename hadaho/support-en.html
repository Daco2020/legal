Support

Hadaho retouches only the skin on faces in your photos and videos. It leaves your face shape alone and keeps your originals. If something isn't working, or there's something you'd like to see, let us know.

Contact us

We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

What happens to my original photos? They stay just as they were. Retouched results are saved to Photos as new copies.

Are my photos or videos sent to a server? No. Analysis and retouching both happen on your phone. We can't see your photos or videos.

Hadaho can't save to Photos. On your iPhone, go to Settings > Apps > Hadaho > Photos and allow Hadaho to add photos.

The screen goes dark while a video is being retouched. If you don't touch the screen for 30 seconds while retouching, Hadaho dims it to save battery. Tap the screen and it brightens again. iPhone can't keep retouching once the screen turns off, so the screen won't turn off by itself while retouching is in progress.

I switched to another app while a long video was being retouched. Hadaho picks up near where it left off.

It says no face was found. Hadaho may not find a face that is very small, turned far to the side, or mostly covered. Those photos and videos are left as they are.

Are there ads? No.

How many can I retouch for free? Your first 10 photos or videos are free. That's 10 in total, not 10 a month, and only the ones saved to Photos count. After that, buy the lifetime pass once and retouch as many as you like. It's not a subscription. If you first downloaded Hadaho on or before December 31, 2026, it's free for you for life.

How do I restore my lifetime pass? In the menu, open Lifetime pass and tap Restore purchases. It also works on another iPhone signed in with the same Apple Account.

Can my family use it? Yes. With Family Sharing, your family members can use your lifetime pass without buying their own.

How do I get a refund? Apple handles payments and refunds. To request a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

You'll find more details in our privacy policy.
