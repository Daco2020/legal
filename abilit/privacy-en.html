Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026

Weuse ("we") respects the privacy of Abilit ("the app") users. This policy explains what leaves your device, why, and what your rights are.

Abilit is an on-device app by design. Your tasks, ability names, and gemstone records are stored only on your device and in your personal iCloud — they are never sent to our servers. Only the items listed below ever leave the app.

1. What we collect and when

CategoryItemsWhen
Usage statisticsA random identifier generated on your device, event timestamps, platform (iOS·iPadOS·macOS), country code, app version, point values, ability slot number (1–3), completion hourAutomatically, on app launch and task creation/completion
Support & feedbackThe message you write, an optional email address, app name/version, OS version, device modelOnly when you send feedback from Settings

What we do not collect: name, phone number, address, payment details, advertising identifiers (IDFA), location. Task titles and ability names are never transmitted. Internal record IDs (UUIDs) are not sent either; abilities are counted only by slot number (1–3).

The random identifier is stored only on your device and disappears when you delete the app. It does not sync between devices, cannot identify you on its own, and is never combined with your name, email, or any other data.

2. Why we process it

  • Usage statistics: to learn which features are actually used and improve the app
  • Support & feedback: to answer your inquiry and improve the app

We never use your data for advertising, profiling, or tracking, and we never sell it.

3. Retention

All items in section 1 are kept for one year from collection, then deleted without delay. Retention is enforced in code: the server automatically deletes expired records every day.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not share your data with third parties.

5. Processors

ProcessorTaskRetention
Cloudflare, Inc.Receiving and storing usage statistics (Workers · D1)Same as section 3
Discord Inc.Receiving support messagesSame as section 3

International transfer: both processors' servers are located in the United States, so the items above are transferred there over the network, for the purposes and retention periods stated above. We confirm that both processors provide protections equivalent to this policy.

You can refuse this transfer. Usage statistics can be turned off anytime in Settings → Privacy → "Send anonymous usage data"; nothing is sent from that moment. The fact that you opted out (the identifier and the opt-out flag) is transmitted once so we can exclude you from statistics — nothing is sent afterwards. Support messages are sent only when you choose to send them.

6. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing at any time by emailing dacokim32@gmail.com. We respond within 10 days of receipt.

The random identifier alone does not let us find a specific user. If you delete the app, the identifier is gone and past records can no longer be attributed to any device; to delete records we already hold, email us at the address above.

Tasks, abilities, and gemstone records on your device are not held by us — you can erase them yourself in Settings → Reset data.

7. Deletion

Data past its retention period, or no longer needed, is deleted without delay using irreversible methods. We keep no paper copies.

8. Security measures

  • All transmission is encrypted with HTTPS
  • Retention is enforced in code — expired records are deleted automatically every day
  • Minimal collection — task titles and ability names are never transmitted by design
  • Access to collected data is limited to the minimum number of people
  • You can turn off usage statistics yourself at any time
  • The analytics identifier lives only on your device and is deleted with the app

9. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 14. The feedback form only sends what a user types in; guardians may request deletion of anything a child sent by emailing dacokim32@gmail.com.

10. Contact

  • Data protection contact: the founder, dacokim32@gmail.com

We answer privacy questions, complaints, and remedy requests without delay.

11. For EU/EEA users (GDPR)

If you use the app in the EU/EEA, the following also applies.

  • Legal basis (Art. 6 GDPR): usage statistics rely on our legitimate interest in

improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f)) or on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)); you can stop transmission anytime in Settings. Support messages rely on your consent when you choose to contact us.

  • Your rights: in addition to section 6, you may request restriction of processing

and data portability, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

  • International transfers: transfers to the processors in section 5 (United States)

are covered by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

  • EU representative (Art. 27): our processing is occasional, small-scale, and

involves no special categories of data, so we consider the exemption from appointing a representative to apply. Contact dacokim32@gmail.com for anything GDPR-related.

12. Changes to this policy

Changes are announced through the app or this page at least 7 days before they take effect — 30 days for changes unfavorable to users.
