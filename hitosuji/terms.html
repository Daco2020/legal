What the app does

You photograph a page you have marked, the app reads the marked sentence, and you turn it into a card or a short video. Everything is processed on your phone.

The app is free to use, with a daily limit on the cards you make and a small Hitosuji watermark in the corner of each one. Videos have no limit.
