Frequently asked

It missed my highlighter. Shoot the page flat, with light coming from the side you are not blocking. A shadow across the page is what most often hides a mark. If the app finds nothing, you can still tap any sentence to pick it.

It picked the wrong words. Drag either end of the selection to stretch it, or tap Fix text on the card screen and correct it by hand.

My paragraph is too long for one card. Tap Split into several under the card. The paragraph is cut at sentence endings, and the cards share one source line.

Where did my card go? Cards are saved to your photo library and kept in the app's shelf, grouped by book.

Is it free? Yes. You can make cards for free, with a daily limit and a small Hitosuji watermark in the corner of each one. Hitosuji Lifetime lifts the limit, and removes that watermark or replaces it with a short line of your own. You buy it once, and nothing renews.

I bought Hitosuji Lifetime but it has not unlocked. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Do my photos go anywhere? No. Reading the page happens on your phone. The only thing that leaves it is the text you type to look up a book. See the privacy policy.
