Yarr is a camera that shoots with sound effects. Tell us if something breaks or if you have an idea.

How to reach us

Inside the app, tap Menu (≡) > Contact us. You can decorate the card with stickers before you send it.

Email works too: weuse.support@gmail.com

We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked

Is it really free? The camera, the sounds, and the basic stickers are free. One purchase unlocks the rest for good. It is not a subscription.

Can I shoot silently? Where local rules allow it, yes. In places that require a shutter sound, such as Korea and Japan, Yarr follows that rule.

Do my photos go anywhere? No. There is no account and no server of ours. Photos, videos, and recordings stay on your device. The privacy policy has the details.

How do I restore a purchase? Menu > Yarr Pro, then tap restore. It comes back on a new device as long as you use the same Apple Account.

What is the watermark? Photos taken on the free plan wear a small Yarr badge, and you can pick its style. Going Pro removes it.

Someone sent me a sticker pack. Now what? Tap the file you received. Yarr opens with a preview, and adding it drops the stickers into your face sticker list. Receiving and using them is free.
